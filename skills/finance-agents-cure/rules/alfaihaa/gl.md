# General ledger rules (Al Faihaa)

- Closing entries of prepaid rent (unearned revenue 2123001) reverse to rental income 4300003, not 4300001. A known wrong booking stays among the re-checked findings.
- Lease audit checks per contract whether water / electricity was recharged to the tenant at contract start or due dates (annually for some tenants, with each instalment for others).
- The pharmacy is leased out: only the drug-sales margin is recognised, as other revenue 4200027.
- Year-end estimated expenses in a period comparison: the comparison year uses its own actual year-end entries × months/12.
- Non-recurring vouchers listed in the site's normalization list can be excluded with a switch, never silently.
